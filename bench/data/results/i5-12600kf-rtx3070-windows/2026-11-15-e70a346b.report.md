## Summary

All four runs completed at 1920×1061. Play frame time improved from 19.4 to 9.4 ms at the median and from 85.8 to 29.3 ms at the 99th percentile on the second run. Both repetitions are included.

In each base-switch run, Meitou's reported camera pivot and follow target moved to member 2, back to member 1, then to 2 and 1 again. The two characters stood about 119,180 world units apart. The camera matched the selected character's X/Z coordinates to the reported tenth of a unit after every switch. The initial save camera looked across base 1, about 536 units from its character.

## Run conditions

Windows Task Scheduler launched the official kit on the interactive desktop. An independent observer queried camera and selection state during the two base-switch runs. The frozen save passed the kit's checks, and the derived-data cache on D: had been used on earlier attempts.

An earlier attempt had exhausted Windows commit during orbit. For this run Windows had an additional active 8 GiB page file on D: with a 16 GiB maximum. The lowest sampled commit headroom was 3,477 MiB. The desktop was 1920×1080 and the measured window was 1920×1061 throughout.
