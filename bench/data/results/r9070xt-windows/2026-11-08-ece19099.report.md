- Windows 10. SendInput worked: 4 of 4 keys in both base-switch runs.

- I ran this build twice. The first set ran first in the session, right after `build.py`, and was hitchy: play p99 42.3 and 17.5 ms, base switch p99 42.7 and 37.6 ms. Since the commits since v0.17.0 are UI scrollbars, AI and docs, I ran it again after the other two targets, and these are those runs. - GPU total p50 is back to 2.4 ms in play (2.6 to 2.7 ms in base switch), as in my 664a81df and 5b656a9e rows; the 3.5 to 4.0 ms I saw on 2026-11-07 for e4a7294a and 010d2eaf did not come back. The frame time is CPU-bound.
