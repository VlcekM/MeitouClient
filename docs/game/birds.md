# Birds

Flocks of birds in the sky (a3-k021). Meitou: `BirdFlockData` (data), `BirdFlocks` (flight, `Meitou.Game`), drawn as loose meshes by `DebrisRenderer` (`Birds.UpdateBirds`, `WorldFrame.BirdPieces`).

## Original (decompile of kenshi_x64.exe)

- **Verified**: `BirdManager` is a singleton (`FUN_140262270`, 0x170 bytes, constructor `FUN_140260f50`) on its own thread named "Birds" (kicked with the real dt by `FUN_1403beeb0`, see [game loop](game-loop.md)); its flight step is `FUN_140262830`, its loader `FUN_140261090`.
- **Verified** (loader): every WILDLIFE_BIRDS (68) record becomes a flock kind with `speed`, `height`, `ground height`, `ground strength`(+0x3c is set to 100 in code), `contour strength`, `distance`, `spawn height min/max`, `scale min/max`, `spawn count`, `flocking`, `circling`, `mesh`, `texture`. The mesh is made with the Ogre mesh manager, the material is a clone of `HWBirds` (`data/materials/deferred/birds.material`: hardware-instanced static mesh, the standard object fragment program, no wing animation in the vertex shader `birds.hlsl`) with the record's `texture` as `DiffuseMap`.
- **Verified**: where a kind flies comes from the BIOME_GROUP `birds` reference list: per group an amount (the reference's first value; 2 to 15 in the install's data) kept in a map keyed by the group's area colour (`index`). `FUN_140260620` sums, over the weather cells (64 x 64, 4608 units) within `distance` of the camera (clamped to the map), the amount of every cell whose group lists the kind. `FUN_140262830` spawns a flock when `birds alive + spawn count <= that sum`.
- **Verified** (`FUN_140262500`, `FUN_1402622d0`): a spawn picks one matching cell at random, a random point in it at the terrain height, and refuses it within 1000 units of the camera controller's position or when the terrain height is not strictly between `spawn height min` and `max`. The flock's birds start there (each 5 units further along a random direction), state 2, facing the camera focus, timer random 30 to 180. Scale is random `scale min` to `scale max`.
- **Verified** (`FUN_140262830`, constants read from the data section; Ogre `Frustum::_getCachedFrustumPlanes`): the thread step (real dt x a time scale of 1.0 at `BirdManager+0xc0`, **Unknown** whether anything else writes it) moves each bird `speed x dt` along its heading, then:
  - a bird over a cell whose group does not list the kind goes to state 1 (return), timer 1e6, target = where it was; back over a listed cell its timer is zeroed;
  - states: 1 return (aims at the target offset sideways by the target's z distance), 2 wander, 3 follow a mate, 4 circle (target creeps along the terrain's slope), 5 circle an attractor, 6 rest (as 2); 8 is never set. Wander and rest steer along the terrain's contour (`contour strength`) and away from or towards `ground height` on slopes (normal.y under 0.999), then look for a mate (flocking kinds: the nearest bird within 100, not circling, that moves away from this one, never one that already follows it); follow aims 100 units beside the mate on its own side and adds `(1 + 0.003 x clamp(lead, -100, 100)) x dt` units of forward speed;
  - height: under 30 above the ground the climb is a flat 5 (vertical component of the wanted direction); else the wanted height is `(1 + 0.3 x f) x height` (f in [-0.5, 0.5) from the bird's index in the group), or the mate's height plus 5 f, and the vertical component is `(wanted - actual) / 2000`; the wanted direction is normalised, and the heading turns by `dt / angle` of the way to it (about one radian per second; snaps within cos 0.99; pi when opposite); the orientation is `Quaternion::FromAxes(up x forward, forward x x, forward)`;
  - timers: expiry of 1, 4 or 6: state 2, timer 10 to 40, new mate within 1000; of 2 or 3: if the kind does not circle, timer 5 to 30 and a new mate within 1000, else state 4 (target = here, altitude = ground, radius 400 to 600, timer 30 to 180); of 5: state 6, timer 10 to 40, leave the attractor;
  - a bird beyond the kind's `distance` (a radius in world units: 500000 covers the map, so birds go on flying) from the camera node and more than 10 units outside any of the six frustum planes is removed (state 0).
- **Verified** (`FUN_140263700`, `FUN_140260e20`, `FUN_140260880`, `FUN_1402604f0`): a BUILDING, MAP_FEATURES or ANIMAL_CHARACTER record's `bird attractor` references (target a WILDLIFE_BIRDS kind, values `r` and `n`) make each placed object an attractor of strength `100 r^2` taking up to `n` birds. A wandering or circling bird takes the attractor with the highest `strength / distance^2` above 1 that is not full: state 5, timer 30 to 90, circle radius 400 to 600 round it. In the install `r` is 100 to 1000 (an attraction radius of 1000 to 10000 units), `n` 1 to 50.
- **Verified**: the mesh is made with the Ogre mesh manager, the material is a clone of `HWBirds` (`data/materials/deferred/birds.material`, hardware instancing, no wing animation) with the record's `texture` as `DiffuseMap`. Each bird is an `InstancedEntity` on a scene node scaled by the bird's scale (`FUN_140260020`), so the mesh's origin is at the bird's position (birdtype1's bounds run y -3.3 to 7.7: it sits on the ground when the step clamps it there). Meitou draws the mesh as a loose piece with that texture on every part (`DebrisPiece.TextureMark`), offset by its bounds middle so the origin lands on the bird (#409: drawn by its middle, a grounded bird sank into the floor).
- **Observed** (install data): the base record `WILDLIFE_BIRDS2218` and Newwworld's `BowlBirdsTEST` use `data/meshes/birdtype1.mesh` (about 26 x 5.5 x 7.5 units, wings along x), 10 birds, speed 220 to 500, height 200 to 800, `distance` 500000.

## Meitou

- `BirdFlocks` (`Meitou.Game`) is the step above, run by `Birds.UpdateBirds` on the display's real time (the original's thread takes the real dt too), so birds do not stop with the game paused and are not part of the simulation or the saves. The terrain normal comes from finite differences of the height 16 units apart (the original asks Ogre's terrain plugin).
- Attractors (`Birds.UpdateAttractors`): the player's completed buildings (snapshot), the level's buildings (not flagged incomplete by their state; destroyed ones count, **Unknown** whether the original skips them), the furniture of their layouts and every map feature (`WorldObjects.BirdAttractorSources`, read once per world off the main thread), and every animal (`CharacterSnapshot.RecordId`; the position follows it each frame, as the original reads its object's position every step). Meitou registers the whole level at once; the original only the loaded zones (**Unknown** how far; the pull falls with distance squared, so only matters for a camera far from the squads).
- The frustum is the view-projection of the game camera, planes normalised.
- Thread (engine choice, as the original's own "Birds" thread): in the interactive game `BirdThread` runs the step beside the frame (stills and duels run it inline); the host starts a step when the last one is done, shows its flocks one frame late and gives the step the terrain heights through `TerrainHeights` (a snapshot taken on the render thread). The host's share is the `birds` profiler lap (about 0.05 ms with 857 birds; the step itself about 0.5 ms, off the render thread). Each kind keeps a per-cell amount table and summed-area tables, so the spawn test is O(1).
